Sign convention (Module 7): x is measured from the left end. Loads P and w(x) are entered positive downward; an applied couple M0 is positive counter-clockwise. Internal shear V is positive when it tends to rotate the segment clockwise; internal moment M is positive when it bends the beam upward (“smiles”). Hence dV/dx = −w, dM/dx = V.

Reactions and diagrams are computed with Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (stiffness method, uniform EI), so statically indeterminate beams are handled too; for determinate beams the result is identical to the statics in the notes. Values at a jump are listed as left/right of the point. Solid dots on the diagrams mark key points; “pure bending” marks regions where V = 0 and M ≠ 0.

Cut position x [m]

FBD approach (left): the segment to the left of the cut is isolated, with the unknown V and M drawn in their positive directions on the cut face; equilibrium gives their values. Graphical approach (right): the shaded area under w between the left end and the cut is the drop in shear, ΔV = −∫w dx, and the shaded area under V is the change in moment, ΔM = ∫V dx; point loads and couples add jumps. Where V crosses zero, M has a local maximum or minimum.
