Pick the point (cylindrical or Cartesian coordinates) and the orientation of the element, and read the stresses from superposition: σ<sub>x</sub> = N/A − My/I, torsion τ = Tρ/J and transverse shear τ = VQ/(It). Sign convention follows the Modules 7–8 notes: +N is tension, +V tends to rotate the element clockwise, +M makes the member "smile", +T is right-handed about +x; on Mohr's circle a shear that rotates the element clockwise is plotted upward.
