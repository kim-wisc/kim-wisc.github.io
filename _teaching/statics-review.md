Statics is the prerequisite that Mechanics of Materials uses on every page: free-body diagrams, support reactions, internal forces by sectioning, distributed-load resultants, centroids and moments of area. Each tab reviews one of these with an interactive example and ends with a few problems to check yourself. Conventions follow the lecture notes: <i>x</i> right, <i>y</i> up, moments positive counter-clockwise, internal axial force drawn in tension.
