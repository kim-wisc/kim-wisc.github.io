Build a beam with any supports and loads, then read off the reactions and the shear-force and bending-moment diagrams. Sign convention follows the Module 7 notes: loads are entered positive downward, an internal shear that rotates the segment clockwise is positive, and a moment that makes the beam "smile" is positive, so d<i>V</i>/d<i>x</i> = −<i>w</i> and d<i>M</i>/d<i>x</i> = <i>V</i>. Statically indeterminate beams are solved with uniform <i>EI</i>.
